Featured Work

HP Capstone: Transforming Data into Commercial Strategy

9-Month Capstone Project | MSBA Program | 2025-2026

During my MSBA program, I partnered with HP on a 9-month capstone project focused on one core challenge: how can data drive better cross-market decision-making?

Through exploratory analysis and performance profiling, I discovered key insights around customer segments, growth opportunities, and operational gaps. I built an interactive dashboard to track performance metrics in real-time, enabling the sales team to spot trends and take action.

But data alone doesn't drive decisions. I worked closely with HP's commercial strategy team to translate findings into actionable recommendations. By identifying high-ROI customer segments and uncovering operational improvements, we informed strategic decisions that shaped HP's commercial strategy.

This project reinforced what I've learned throughout my career: the most powerful analytics work translates data into a narrative that drives action.
